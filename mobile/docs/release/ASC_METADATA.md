```
Practice the civics questions for the U.S. naturalization interview — free, offline, and in your own language.

It drills the official civics question banks in a simple multiple-choice format so you can review at your own pace.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 2025 civics test — 128 questions, 20 asked, 12 to pass
• 2008 civics test — 100 questions, 10 asked, 6 to pass, for applications filed before October 20, 2025
• 65/20 starred subsets for both versions, for applicants 65+ with 20 years as a permanent resident
• 50/20 and 55/15 routes for applicants exempt from the English requirement
• Your state's capital question, answered for the state you pick

FOURTEEN LANGUAGES
English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Korean, Hindi, Haitian Creole, Thai, Lao, Hmong, Burmese, Portuguese, and Russian. Every question and answer appears in your language with the English underneath, so you learn the English you will actually need in the room.

READ ALOUD
Tap to hear any question or answer spoken in your language, using the voices already on your device. If your device has no voice for that language, the text is read in English and the app tells you so — it is never silent.

DAILY REMINDER, IF YOU WANT ONE
Off by default. A local notification scheduled by your own device — no account, no server.

ANSWERS THAT CHANGE
Some answers change after an election or appointment — President, Vice President, Speaker of the House, Chief Justice, your senators, your governor. This app deliberately does not print those names, because a name memorized from a stale app is a wrong answer in the interview. Those questions appear as study cards pointing you to uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates to check the current answer.

WORKS OFFLINE
Every question is stored inside the app. No connection is needed to practice.

NO ACCOUNT, NO DATA COLLECTION
There is no sign-up, no login, no advertising, and no tracking. Your language, your state, and your answers stay on your device for the current session and are gone when you close the app. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored.

IMPORTANT NOTICES

FormN400.org is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, authorized, endorsed, or approved by USCIS, DHS, or any U.S. government agency.

We cannot provide legal advice. For immigration legal questions, consult a licensed attorney or accredited representative.

For official study materials, see uscis.gov.
```
